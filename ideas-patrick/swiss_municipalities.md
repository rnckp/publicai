# 20 varied municipalities in German-speaking Switzerland

Selected on 24 September 2026. This is a deliberately varied selection, not a statistically representative sample. It covers 12 cantons and contrasts population scale, urban and rural settings, economic roles, and social contexts. Size descriptions are qualitative, not official statistical classifications.

All links lead to public-authority websites rather than tourism portals. German-speaking municipalities in multilingual cantons are included.

## Selection notes and sources

- **Demographic contrast:** The selection juxtaposes metropolitan, suburban, rural, and resort communities. These are qualitative selection criteria, not a ranking by income, age, or nationality. Spreitenbach's municipality reports that more than half its residents are foreign nationals in its [population update](https://www.spreitenbach.ch/aktuelles/aktuelles/news-detail/artikel/spreitenbach-ist-leicht-gewachsen/). Zermatt's [official portrait](https://gemeinde.zermatt.ch/unser-dorf) describes about 6,000 residents from 60 nations and a community strongly connected to tourism.
- **Population scale:** Zürich anchors the large-city end; Trub provides a small-community contrast. Trub's [official facts page](https://www.trub.ch/leben-in-trub/zahlen-und-fakten) lists 1,273 residents at 31 December 2023. This dated figure illustrates scale and is not presented as a current population estimate.
- **Basel:** The linked Basel-Stadt portal is the relevant official authority website for the city; the canton performs the city's municipal administration.
- **Appenzell:** The selected unit is **Bezirk Appenzell**, a municipal-level district in Appenzell Innerrhoden, rather than the whole canton or the wider settlement.
- **Geographical coverage:** The selection spans northwestern, northeastern, central, and Alpine German-speaking Switzerland, including the German-speaking areas of Graubünden and Valais.
- **Website verification:** Official authority pages were checked through live retrieval or indexed official-site results on 24 September 2026. The websites linked in the table are also the starting points for municipal portraits and further local statistics.
